# Gate 1(d) Launch-Scope Decision

- **Decision date:** 2026-08-11
- **Controller:** Laxmi Narayana Bingi, operating personally without a
  registered company, LLC, or organization
- **Operator establishment:** Illinois, United States
- **Initial active market:** United States
- **EEA/UK posture:** Active marketing is deferred until the applicable Gate
  1(d) processor, transfer, operational-evidence, and legal-review work is
  complete.

## Decision

ResumeAI will use a phased launch. The initial launch may remain generally
accessible on the public internet, but the owner will not intentionally target
EEA or UK users through region-specific advertising, paid search targeting,
localized storefronts or listings, country-specific pricing, or claims that the
service is offered specifically in those regions.

Before beginning active EEA/UK marketing, the owner will re-open this decision
and complete the applicable items in `docs/GATE1D_OWNER_ACTIONS.md`, including:

- processor agreements and international-transfer evidence;
- vendor region, retention, subprocessor, and privacy-setting evidence;
- the live privacy-rights/deletion drill and consent-network proof;
- the incident tabletop, DPIA approval, and qualified legal review; and
- any required representative, DPO, or certified-CMP assessment.

## Boundary

This decision records launch intent; it is not a legal conclusion that a
particular law can never apply. Incidental access from another region is not an
authorization to start targeted marketing there. The decision must be reviewed
before any EEA/UK campaign or material regional product change.

## Owner approval

The owner approved this US-focused phased-launch decision on 2026-08-11.
